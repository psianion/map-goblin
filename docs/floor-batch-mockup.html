Batch 3 · floors & terrain

Six floors to rebuild first

Walls are done — Fieldstone and Palisade replaced theirs. Floors are next, and the pack currently ships 22 of them: five overlapping stone tiles, four browns that are nearly the same brown, five wood tints, two grasses. These six cover what a DM actually paints, and retire fifteen of the twenty-two on the way.

  • 22 floor entries today
  • 6 families proposed
  • 15 retired into them
  • 200px per grid cell, fixed

The candidates

Each specimen is the real texture shipping in dungeon-classic today, tiled four times so the repeat is visible — the same way it reads on a map, not the way it reads as a swatch.

1

Dressed Stone Floor

large-flagstone-a-01 · 4×4 cells · 800px

one tile
Why this one
The single most-painted surface in a dungeon interior, and the floor the Fieldstone walls stand on. Whatever this looks like is what most maps look like.
What's wrong now
Substance-render flatness. Uniform overhead lighting, no ink on the joint lines, grey-brown with nowhere for torchlight to catch.
Target
Cut slabs with painted chips and wear, thin ink on every joint, warm value break so lit and shadowed stone read apart.

Retires rectangular-tiles-a-01 rock-tiles-b-01 smooth-stone-floor-a-10

2

Cave Rock Floor

cave-floor-06-a · 5×5 cells · 1000px

one tile
Why this one
The other half of "dungeon". Every cavern, mine and undercroft sits on this, and it has to read as *not* built by hands.
What's wrong now
Lowest legibility of the set. Pebble mush at one frequency; at table zoom it collapses into a flat brown field with no shapes to read.
Target
Two frequencies — large worn boulder forms carrying the composition, fine grit between them. Ink only on the big forms.

Retires rock-ground-c-06

3

Packed Earth

dirt-b-04 · 5×5 cells · 1000px

one tile
Why this one
The base layer. It sits under and blends into everything else, so its value and warmth set the key for every other terrain in the pack.
What's wrong now
Featureless brown noise — no structure at any scale. Nothing here was drawn; it was generated.
Target
Painted scuff and trodden variation, faint stone poking through, a warm-to-cool range wide enough to blend against both grass and stone.

Retires dirt-c-02 cracked-dirt-a-01 grassy-dirt-a-02 gravel-06-c gravel-06-j

4

Grass

grass-a-01 · 6×6 cells · 1200px

one tile
Why this one
The outdoor default, and the hardest test in the batch — organic, no repeating structure to hide behind. If the painterly style holds here it holds anywhere.
What's wrong now
The worst of the six. A blurred green-brown cloud with no blade structure and no stroke direction. Reads as fog, not ground.
Target
Clumped blade strokes with real direction, darker root shadow under the clumps, occasional earth showing through. Painterly, not noise.

Retires grass-a-09

5

Plank Flooring

Wooden_Flooring_A_Ashen · 5×5 cells · 1000px

one tile
Why this one
Taverns, ships, keeps, upper floors. Also the strongest tiling test in the set: directional structure makes every seam and every repeat obvious.
What's wrong now
Laminate uniformity. Planks are too narrow, identical in width and value, machine-perfect — a flooring sample, not boards someone laid.
Target
Wider boards, varied length and value per board, visible grain and knots, ink in the gaps. Four existing tints collapse to one master plus tint.

Retires Wooden_Flooring_B_Dark Wooden_Flooring_C_Light Wooden_Flooring_D_Red Wooden_Flooring_E_Walnut

6

Cobblestone

cobblestone-a-01 · 3×3 cells · 600px

one tile
Why this one
Roads, courtyards, town squares — the built-exterior surface that bridges dressed stone and open ground.
What's wrong now
Smallest tile in the pack at 3×3, so it repeats twice as often as anything else — and the stones are so small the whole field turns to dark gravel at map zoom.
Target
Rebuilt at 5×5. Fewer, larger, rounder stones with real size variation, ink around each, mortar dark enough to read as gaps.

What the pipeline requires

Not preferences — these are what the existing terrain shader and pack build already assume. A texture that misses them will not tile correctly on a painted map.

ConstraintValueWhy it is fixed
Cell density200 px / cell gridPixels: 200 in the pack config; the splat shader samples at exactly this density.
Tile size5×5 or 6×6 cells 1000–1200px. The shader repeats every cellsWide cells, so a small tile repeats sooner — cobblestone's 3×3 is the current worst.
SeamTileable, all four edges Sampled with wrap. A seam that survives to the pack shows up as a hard grid line across the whole painted area.
Variants3+ per family floors.selectionStrategy: "cell-seeded" already picks per cell. With one variant it has nothing to pick from.
ViewOrthographic top-down No perspective, no cast shadow — the map is lit at runtime and any baked light fights the torches.
StylePainterly, thin ink Per the art style guide, which gates release. Ink on structure edges only, never on noise.

Grey master plus tint, same as the walls. Dressed stone, cave rock and cobblestone get painted once in neutral grey and tinted per material — that is how Fieldstone and Palisade were built, and it is why five wood tints can collapse into one master. Grass and earth are painted in colour; tinting mud into grass has never once worked.

Deliberately not in this batch

Water (2 tiles) Wants animation and a shader pass, not a texture swap. Different problem, own batch.
Gravel (2 tiles) Near-duplicate of packed earth at map zoom. Falls out as an earth variant rather than its own family.
Wood tints (4 tiles) Tinted from the plank master once it exists. No separate generation.
Edges (17 tiles) Cliffs, banks and floor breaks are shaped assets, not tiles. They should follow the floors they border, not lead them.
