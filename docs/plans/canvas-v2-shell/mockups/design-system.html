Canvas v2 design system

The tokens the v2 editor already ships, and every control the new shell needs, drawn in light and dark side by side with the measurements under each. Verify or reject piece by piece. What survives becomes the one component set, built on shadcn primitives, and the old Moss theme and the hand-written v2 CSS go.

Base unit 4px. Three control heights: 24 inside panels and cards, 28 in bars and the rail's small buttons, 36 on the rail. Icon strokes 1.75. One accent: none. Active is a 1.5px inset ring in the ink colour. Nothing is filled except the one primary button and tooltips.

Colour tokens v2.css, verified against the running editor

Rules. Text on panel uses text (1b1b1b) for values, text-2 (4d4d4d) for labels, text-3 (6b6b6b) for captions and keys. Lines are hairline only, 1px, line for rest and line-strong for slider tracks. Danger is reserved for delete. No second accent anywhere.

Type IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Mono

Rules. Sans for words, Mono for numbers, keys, times and ids. Captions are 10px, 500, uppercase, 0.04em tracking. Nothing on the chrome is larger than 13px. Tabular numerals everywhere a number can change width.

Spacing, shape, elevation

Rules. 12px from every screen edge and 12px between floats. 8px between a float's edge and its content, 12px on the left of text rows. 6px between tiles, 2px between icon buttons in a row, 10px between rail buttons. Radius 8 for floats, 6 for controls and tiles, 4 for keycaps and level rows. One shadow for floats, none on controls.

Icon button with key Rail 36, dock 32, bar 28, row 26

Rules. The key letter sits in the bottom right corner, mono, 8px at 28, 9px at 36, in text-3, 3px from the edges at 28 and 5px at 36. When a button has a key its icon moves 1.5px up and left (2px at 36) so the letter owns its corner. Hover is a panel-3 fill. Active is the 1.5px inset ring and no fill, so hover and active can show together. Focus is a 2px outline in the focus token, offset 1px. Disabled is 38% opacity. The icon never moves between states.

Buttons, chips, keys

Controls 24px tall inside panels and cards

Rules. A control is 24px tall in a panel. Segmented controls use panel-3 for the chosen cell, no ring. Switch knob is always white; the track is line-strong off and ink on. Slider thumb is 12px with a 1.5px ink ring, the track 2px. Values sit right-aligned in mono beside the label and the slider drops to a second line.

Tiles and swatches 56 × 44

Setting rows and captions The panel and card vocabulary

Rules. Label left in text-2, control right. A row is 28px minimum. Sections are separated by 12px and a caption. A slider row puts the value beside the label and the track on the next line, full width. Folds collapse to one line with their summary in mono.

Panel header, action row, flyout row

Dock columns

Rules. A column is a caption row then a strip, 8px padding, 10px sides, hairline between columns. The caption's right end holds the column's one control (a dropdown, a plus). Tiles are 56 wide with 6px between. The dock is 112 tall and never changes height between tools.

Bars, rail, status

Overlays Tooltip, menu, dialog, toast

Rules. Tooltips are ink-filled, 24 tall, label plus key. Menus are floats with 28px rows and 6px padding. Dialogs are floats at 320 wide with the primary action last. Toasts are ink-filled with one underlined undo verb and leave by themselves.
