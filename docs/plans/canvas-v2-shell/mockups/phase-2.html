Wave 1. Brushes and floors

1. Terrain brush, Dungeondraft modelTerrain / Terrain brush, panel and dock

  • 1
    Intensity, not Strength. It is a rate: how fast paint builds while the pointer is held. The slider sits in the dock because it changes mid-stroke.
  • 2
    Paint builds up. The three strokes are the same slot held for one, two and four seconds at a low intensity. Nothing is solid after one pass; the organic look comes from holding.
  • 3
    Blending in the panel. Smooth blending off gives the ragged organic edge, on gives the soft one. Fill enclosed floor floods the floor under the pointer with the slot's texture in one click.
  • 4
    Erase is a mode. Paint back to the floor's own texture; the status hint names the held key.
  • 5
    Six slots, Fill beside From library. Right-click empties a slot. Biomes stay out until a pack declares one.

2. Floor opacity and per-floor edge bandSelect / Floor card

  • 1
    Opacity does something. The picked floor is at 50%; the moss shows through it. The slider is the same row as today, it just works.
  • 2
    Edge band per floor. This floor says None, so no ink band is drawn round it; the floor beside it keeps the layer's Cave ink. The panel's Edge band is the default for new floors.

3. Carve outDesign / Floor panel, Mode section

  • 1
    Mode row returns. Draw or Carve out, as round two drew it. Carve out is the visible truth; holding Alt while drawing is the fast path.
  • 2
    A carved rectangle. The dashed shape being drawn subtracts from every floor under it on this layer, as one undo step. The hole shows the ground.
  • 3
    Hint carries the key. Alt carves, as the brushes' hint carries Alt for erase.

4. Node mode with midpoint handles, floor curveSelect / Floor card, Node mode on

  • 1
    Two kinds of handle. Square at every node, small round at every midpoint. Drag a round one and it becomes a node.
  • 2
    Curve on floors. The Edit column's Curve toggle bends the dragged segment on floors the way it does on walls; the bottom edge here is bent.
  • 3
    Hint while in node mode. Enter and Escape leave it, Delete removes a node, Shift drags straight.

Wave 2. Walls and doors

5. Corner styleDesign / Wall, dock

  • 1
    Corner style in the dock. Rounded or Sharp, per wall, next to Type and Width. The wall set's own corner pieces are used, both ship with every set.
  • 2
    Same set, both ways. The left room's corners are rounded, the right room's are sharp.

6. Wall merge and auto-joinDesign / Wall panel; Select with two walls picked

  • 1
    Auto-join in the panel. Drawing to another wall's endpoint joins them into one wall. Off, they stay two walls that touch.
  • 2
    Merge appears for two walls. The Mixed card's header says 2 walls and the action row grows a Merge verb; the floating bar shows it too.

7. Freestanding doorsDesign / Door panel; a freestanding door's card

  • 1
    Freestanding in the panel. On, the next door is placed anywhere, not snapped to a wall edge.
  • 2
    Angle is editable. A freestanding door's card unlocks Angle in 5° steps; an anchored door keeps its wall's angle and the field reads only.
  • 3
    The door in open ground. Drawn at 35° with its swing arc; light blocking follows its state as today.

Wave 3. New terrain tools

8. Water brushTerrain / Water brush, panel and dock

  • 1
    Brush first, shapes later. Size and a Brush or Shape seg in the dock; Shape is drawn disabled until the shape placer lands.
  • 2
    Two colours and a blend distance. Shallow at the edge, Deep inside, blended over the distance in cells. Border draws the thin line along the edge.
  • 3
    The water on the map. A painted pool with the shallow rim, the deep middle and the border on.

9. Material brushTerrain / Material brush, panel and dock

  • 1
    Material swatches in the dock. Each is a pack material with its own border art; Size beside them.
  • 2
    One solid piece. A freehand stroke closes into a patch; on release the material's border units are laid round the outline, "like scatter but fuller". Smooth rounds the outline before the border goes on.

10. PathTerrain / Path, panel and dock; a path's card

  • 1
    Width, Smoothness, Curve, Node mode, pieces. The on-the-fly controls in the dock; the piece swatches are the pack's path units.
  • 2
    Transitions and snap in the panel. Hard, Fade, Grow or Shrink at each end; editable later on the card, unlike Dungeondraft.
  • 3
    Units along the line. Individual pieces laid along the polyline, never a stretched strip, fading in at the start and hard at the end.

  • 2

Wave 5. Map-wide
