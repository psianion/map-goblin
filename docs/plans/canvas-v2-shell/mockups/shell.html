Canvas v2 shell mockup, round two

Design family on the one Floor tool, then the Select tool empty and with a floor picked. Every change from round one is in: keys at the icon corner, outline for active, flyout and panel in one slot, dock holds swatches plus on-the-fly controls, asset panel stops above the dock, help at the bottom right.

Frame A. Design family clicked, flyout open

  • 1
    Keys at the corner. Every icon carries its key as a small letter, rail, flyout, dock and card alike. Keycaps only survive in the status hint.
  • 2
    Active is an outline. Design shows a ring on the rail, no fill, no tint. Same ring on every active tile.
  • 3
    Flyout in the slot. Four tools: Floor, Wall, Door, Elevation. Pick one and this same box becomes that tool's panel.
  • 4
    Dock already follows. Level and layers are there before a tool is picked; the rest fills in on pick.

Frame B. Floor tool, panel in the same slot

  • 1
    Panel replaces the flyout. Breadcrumb says Design / Floor; the switcher row changes tool without reopening the flyout. The panel stays open while you draw.
  • 2
    Panel is setup only. Draw or Carve out, snap, the edge band, default opacity. Nothing here changes mid-shape.
  • 3
    Shape row in the dock. One Floor tool: rectangle, polygon, regular polygon with its Sides stepper beside it. Curve and Node mode are toggles here, not rail modes.
  • 4
    Look column. Colour swatch, the five style presets, the texture strip, and From library which opens the asset panel.
  • 5
    Polygon in progress. The dashed outline is the shape being clicked out; the hint line carries the keys for it.
  • 6
    Help moved. The question mark sits at the end of the status bar and opens the shortcuts sheet.

Frame C. Select, nothing picked

  • 1
    Kind filter first. A box select grabs only the kinds switched on. All is the default; here only Props and Lights are on, so a drag over the room skips the floor.
  • 2
    Not an empty box. The panel says how to pick, and offers Previous tool (X) to bounce back to Floor.
  • 3
    Dock keeps the last family's swatches. Floor's Look column stays so a texture pick is one click away. Shape and Edit columns go, since Select does not draw.

Frame D. Select, a floor picked

  • 1
    The floor card in the slot. Kind and layer in the header, verbs in the action row, then only the fields that survived the audit: colour, texture, tint, opacity, the Fill fold.
  • 2
    Floating action bar stays. Same verbs beside the selection. Node mode (N) lives here too, so a corner drag is one click from the canvas.
  • 3
    Dock highlights the layer. Stone floor is the picked floor's layer. Look column still shows Floor's swatches; clicking a texture applies it to the selection.

Open questions for round three

  1. Carve out as a mode or a modifier. Drawn as a Draw / Carve out segmented control in the panel. The faster alternative is a held key shown in the hint line, like Alt on the brushes. Both?
  2. Where Sides lives when the shape is not Regular. Drawn as hidden until Regular polygon is picked. Keeping it visible but disabled holds the dock width steady.
  3. Edge band on the panel. It affects how a floor's outline is drawn (none, cave ink, a texture's own border). Per floor on the card too, or only as a default here?
  4. Which family next. Terrain with the material brush would exercise the brush pattern. Objects would exercise the asset panel, tags and colour per region.
