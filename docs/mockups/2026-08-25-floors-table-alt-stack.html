Floor Stack (alternate idea)

the building as a dollhouse stack: slide between slabs, ghost tokens bleed through from adjacent floors. Click a tilted slab or the rail.
THE STACKYour floor sits full-size in the middle; the floor above and below tilt away as translucent slabs, live. One click travels the elevator. The building reads as one place, not a list of maps.
GHOST TOKENSFloors in a group share a grid origin, so a party member above or below projects onto your map as a dashed ghost with an up or down arrow, at their true position. Cheap: no second fog pass, just marker sprites.
WHY IT'S TEMPTINGSpatial truth ("Vex is right under us") beats a PiP thumbnail for the split-party feel, and the DM gets whole-building awareness without a second render pipeline.
WHY IT MIGHT LOSETilted slabs cost vertical screen space and need every floor resident in memory; ghost markers leak nothing about fog but do leak position (rule: only party tokens ghost, never NPCs). Tabs+PiP stays the safer v1; this could layer on top later since it shares the same per-seat viewed-scene model.
