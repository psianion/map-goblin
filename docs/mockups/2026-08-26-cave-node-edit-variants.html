Cave wall node editing — two variants

Real geometry: fourteen consecutive band pieces from the Warren's north-west lobe, at their generated positions, with the real gg-demo art and the real measured joints. Drag the handles in both panels. They behave differently on purpose.

A · Handles on the pieces art is the truth, outline follows

Every placed wall asset gets a node handle. Click to select, Shift-click to add, drag to move the whole run. This is today's node-edit interaction pointed at asset children instead of composed stones.

 

B · Handles on the joints kit is the truth, outline snaps

Handles sit where two pieces meet. Drag one and the span re-solves against what the kit can actually build: the outline lands on a chord a real piece spans at scale 1, and the band re-lays with whichever pieces fit. Ghosts preview the choice while you drag.

 

What the kit can build

What A costs

  • Nothing new to learn. Same handles, same Shift-click, same group drag the wall-stone editor already has. It just reads asset children as its node source.
  • The art never degrades. Pieces keep the rotation, scale and nudge the chord-walk gave them; a drag is a translation and nothing is re-chosen.
  • Joints tear. Move a run and its two ends stop meeting their neighbours. The read-out counts open seams. That gap is real, and closing it is hand work.
  • The outline is a follower, not a constraint. With "outline follows" off, the floor and the rock drift apart and sight geometry stops matching the art.

What B costs

  • You cannot drag to an arbitrary shape. The handle lands where the kit can reach, not where the cursor is. That is the point, and it is also the thing to try before committing.
  • Every edit re-picks pieces. A drag can swap a 2×4 straight for a bend plus a 2×2, so the wall's texture changes under your hand, not just its position.
  • Turns are ±90° only. The kit has three straight lengths and eleven bends, all quarter turns; a gentle curve is approximated by chord-walking, exactly as the generator does.
  • Needs the solver in the engine. A is an input change; B moves fitArc-style chord fitting out of gen-goblin-warren.mjs and into the editor.
