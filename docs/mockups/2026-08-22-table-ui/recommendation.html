The Table, reshaped

Recommendation for the session surface. Same data, same modules, different shell: the map owns the viewport, every panel is a popover off a labelled icon rail, and nothing in the chrome scrolls except the log feed.

The rules this layout is built on

  • Nothing scrolls but the log. Every popover is sized to its worst realistic case at 720p (ledger at the bottom). Where a list can outgrow that, it switches to a denser form or gains a filter; it never grows a scrollbar.
  • One popover open at a time, anchored to its icon. Click the icon or press its key to open; click again, Esc, or another icon closes it. The map never shrinks.
  • Play on the map, prep behind an icon. Rooms, doors and tokens are acted on where they are, with a small anchored menu; the rail popovers are the overview, not the only path. Scene, World and Triggers are prep and sit below a divider.
  • Two shells, not one shell with fewer panels. Players get a turn pill, a "Me" card and a roll bar; they never see FPS, tool state, or lists of things they cannot act on.
  • Moss at night, as the editor already runs. Green counts: active tool, current turn, primary action, live state. Everything else is tinted grey. Selection is a raised surface.

Not in this pass

  • Resizable or detachable popovers. Fixed sizes are what make the no-scroll ledger checkable.
  • Day mode for the table. Night is the table's one theme today; Moss night tokens only.
  • Touch layout under 900px. The rail collapses to a bottom strip there; a separate adapt pass.
  • Chat. The roll bar posts rolls and notes as today; it is not a chat system.
