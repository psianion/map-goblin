Recreation contract (from the renderer study)

  • One neutral grey master per design; color comes from the runtime tint, never separate color passes.
  • Ink outline at about half the source kit's weight.
  • Straights tile edge-to-edge with zero horizontal padding; trim vertical padding only.
  • Every straight in a design shares one content-band height; it becomes the design's scale reference.
  • Corners, joints and endings keep their full padded tile: the padding IS the alignment pivot.
  • The renderer auto-places straights, connectors (fan stones), 1×1 corners and endings; joints and large corners are manual-swap showpieces.
