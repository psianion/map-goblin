BEAT 03 · SIGHT · visibility re-sweeps per frame
  • Identity call: Newsreader + Plex are the product's committed families (Moss chrome). The landing keeps them and changes the volume — Newsreader at display optical sizes, italic as the goblin's voice. One product, one typographic self.

Motion spec (GSAP × R3F)

  1. One pinned timeline per beat, ScrollTrigger scrub; camera on a CatmullRom path, eased with ease-out quint. No bounce anywhere — state settles (the app's --ease-settle carries over).
  2. Beat 1 → 2: line-draw via dashoffset on SVG-extruded splines, then wall meshes scale-Y 0→1 staggered along the draw order; torches ignite 120ms apart, light intensity 0→1 with a 2-frame overshoot flicker.
  3. Beat 3: visibility polygon computed per frame from the same wall segments (the product's sweep, miniaturized). It must be real geometry, not a baked video, or the claim rings false.
  4. Beat 4: pane split via viewport scissor, not two canvases.
  5. Beat 5: scroll maps to clock; ambient color + shadow azimuth lerp; rain = instanced quads, on-demand only.
  6. Reduced motion: every beat has a composed still; scroll becomes hard cuts between them, the same rule as the app's fog reveals. Content never gated on animation.
  7. Mobile: pre-rendered scrubbed image sequence of the same choreography; the WebGL scene never ships to phones.
  8. Perf floor: DPR clamp at 1.5, frameloop="demand" outside pinned ranges, single scene graph reused across beats (one diorama the whole way; that's the point).

Hard constraints carried from the repo

  • Licensing: dungeon-classic pack art is FA-derived and non-redistributable. Nothing from it (including raw app screenshots) goes on a public page. The diorama's materials are bespoke to the page; product-accurate textures wait on forge output.
  • Art style gate applies: painterly + ink outline post-pass (inverted hull), matte everything, one global shadow direction, black negative space with torn edge. If a frame reads as "Unity demo," it fails the gate.
  • Naming: Good Goblin everywhere; "the Editor" / "the Table" capitalized as proper surfaces; map-goblin never appears.
