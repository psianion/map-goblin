Where does the page live: in the dark, on the table, or in between?

Three directions, two stills each: the hero and the nightfall beat, because nightfall is where they differ most. Typography, voice, camera choreography, and copy are identical across all three; only the world around the diorama changes. Each frame shows its own answer for the molten-green seam.

Pick one: D1, D2, or D3. The art pass is scoped to the winner.

What stays constant · what the pick decides

CONSTANT IN ALL THREE

  • Type & voice — Newsreader display, Plex body/mono, goblin marginalia in italic.
  • Camera & beats — same scroll choreography, same eight beats, same copy.
  • Goblin green #B6D648 — marketing accent only; never enters the app.
  • S1–S4 structural fixes — copy/scene sync, trust-beat rebuild, painted tokens, grain fix land regardless.

DECIDED BY THE PICK

  • Seam material & routing — void vein (D1) / wood crack (D2) / stone inlay (D3); per-beat routing folds into this.
  • Beat 1 background — ink strokes draw themselves in every direction; on D2 they are literally ink on the parchment.
  • Night beat behavior — always-dark world (D1) vs performed nightfall (D2) vs local dusk (D3).
  • Contrast rules — light directions: ink on parchment/stone ≥ 4.5:1; green used as readable ink (#5F7A14), never pale fill.
