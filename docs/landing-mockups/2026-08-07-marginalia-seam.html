Recommendation: Variant B, with C's bleed stolen for one beat. B keeps everything the idea is good at — a persistent narrative spine, a diegetic origin for the marginalia, the door payoff — while spending green the way the brand already spends it: rationed, so that when it moves you look. Steal C's under-skirt bleed for the scene-swap beat only (the incoming map rising over the seam is the one place "light from beneath" says something).

The honest risk. A full-height glowing stripe is one bad tuning away from three failure modes: it reads as a decorative divider (or worse, a scrollbar), it competes with the CTA for the only saturated green on the page, and molten-green liquid drifts the register from "feral goblin" toward "toxic sludge." B avoids all three structurally: the seam is dark 90% of its length so it reads as damage in the surface rather than a stripe on the page; the lit segment is small, mobile, and never adjacent to the CTA at rest; and cooled-obsidian-with-embers reads geological, not chemical. If the pulse still fights the CTA in the build, dim the pulse — never the CTA.
